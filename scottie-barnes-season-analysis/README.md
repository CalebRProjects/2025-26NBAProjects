# Overview

This project examines Scottie Barnes as a two-way engine and evaluates his case as one of the league’s most impactful defenders during the 2025–26 season.

With added offensive help, Barnes has shifted more of his energy toward defense, leading a Raptors unit that has emerged as a top-five defense. The analysis focuses on how his all-around skill set: passing, versatility, and defensive disruption—translates into real team impact.

Rather than viewing him as a scorer, this project frames Barnes through the lens of two-way value and possession-level influence.

# Purpose

The goal of this project is:

* Evaluate Barnes’ impact as a two-way player

* Identify how his offensive role functions within a team context

* Analyze defensive value through event creation and matchup difficulty

* Examine how his skill set translates to team-level success

* Assess his legitimacy as a DPOY candidate

# Method & Data

* NBA game logs (via hoopR) for season trends

* Playtype efficiency (offense + defense)

* Shot zone data across multiple seasons

* Defensive metrics (stocks, deflections, PPP allowed)

* On-ball matchup workload and efficiency

* Team defensive performance (DRTG context)

# Why This Matters

**For Player Evaluation**

Barnes’ value is not tied to scoring volume. It comes from his ability to facilitate offense while impacting nearly every defensive possession.

**For Offensive Role**

He functions as a connector and secondary creator. His passing drives offense, while scoring comes primarily in advantage situations.

**For Defensive Impact**

His combination of on-ball defense, help activity, and event creation is elite. He ranks near the top of the league in stocks and deflections while taking on primary matchups (see defensive data on pages 3–4).

**For Team Impact**

Toronto’s top-five defense is built around his versatility and consistency. His ability to handle difficult assignments while creating turnovers directly drives team success.

**For Awards Context**

Given his workload, efficiency, and impact, Barnes has a legitimate case as one of the most valuable defensive players in the league.
