# Overview

This project examines Gui Santos’s unexpected emergence as a meaningful contributor for Golden State during a difficult stretch of the 2025-26 season.

With key players unavailable, Santos was forced into a larger role under poor team conditions. Rather than simply increasing volume, he showed real growth: improving efficiency, expanding his offensive responsibilities, and developing into a more complete player.

The analysis focuses on how his production scaled with opportunity and whether that growth is sustainable within a competitive rotation.

# Purpose

The goal of this project is:

* Evaluate whether Santos’ production increase reflects real development
* Identify how his role shift impacted efficiency and usage
* Analyze changes in shot profile and offensive approach
* Assess defensive growth and activity
* Determine whether he projects as a long-term rotation piece

# Method & Data
* Game splits (early season vs expanded role)
* Per-75 possession normalization to control for minutes
* Shot distribution and free-throw rate trends
* Efficiency metrics (TS%, relative TS%)
* Defensive activity (deflections, impact metrics)
* Multi-year development snapshot

# Why This Matters

**For Player Evaluation** 

Santos did not just scale up production with minutes. He improved efficiency while taking on a larger role, a strong sign of real development (page 2).

**For Offensive Role**

His game has shifted toward rim pressure and better shot selection. Increased downhill attacks and free-throw generation highlight a more sustainable offensive game (page 3).

**For Skill Profile**

He fits the mold of a modern role player: high three-point volume, strong rim presence, and minimal wasted midrange attempts (shot distribution on page 4).

**For Defensive Impact**

His activity has translated into real defensive value, with strong deflection rates and improving impact metrics over his years in the league (pages 5–6).

**For Team Context**

In a season that has gone off track, Santos is a developmental win. He's shown he can contribute within a functional system and potentially hold a role in future rotations.
