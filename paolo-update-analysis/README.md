# Overview

This project examines Paolo Banchero’s recent scoring surge through a simple question: is this stretch a real step forward, or just a hot run?

Over his last 10 games, Banchero has been far more efficient while Orlando has won consistently. But the jumper remains inconsistent, and the roster context has been unstable due to injuries.

Instead of focusing on the results, this analysis looks at how his offense is being created. The goal is to determine whether changes in shot selection and playstyle are driving the efficiency, and whether those changes are sustainable.

# Purpose

The goal of this project is:

* Identify what’s actually driving Banchero’s recent efficiency

* Track changes in shot diet, especially rim vs midrange usage

* Evaluate how much of his scoring comes from foul drawing and rim pressure

* Use playtype data to understand where his offense is most effective

* Connect individual production to lineup context and team performance

# Method & Data

* NBA API (via hoopR) for game logs and splits

* Shot distribution comparing early season vs recent stretch

* Free throw rate and league-wide context

* Synergy playtype data (PPP, frequency, efficiency)

* 3-man lineup combinations to evaluate fit and impact

# Why This Matters

## For Player Evaluation

Banchero’s efficiency isn’t coming from improved shooting, it’s coming from playing to his strengths. His value shows up through rim pressure, physicality, and playmaking off defensive attention.

## For Offensive Role

He is most effective when attacking downhill and operating in structured actions, not when relying on difficult jump shots.

## For Team Construction

Orlando’s best lineups around him combine spacing and defensive stability. His production scales best when those conditions are met.

## For Interpreting the Stretch

This run is less about a “breakout” and more about alignment. If the shot profile holds, the efficiency is real. If not, it likely regresses.
